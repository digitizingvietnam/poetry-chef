# Glossary

**Emotion** (Cảm xúc, gánh nặng)
An abstract feeling the user wants to "cook", e.g. *cô đơn*, *nhung nhớ*. It replaces the main ingredient of an Original Recipe.

**Weight**
How heavy the Emotion feels to the user, expressed as a mass (e.g. *5 kg*). It flavours the Re-cooked Recipe but never changes which Original Recipe is chosen.

**Original Recipe** (Công thức gốc)
A recipe published in a historical Vietnamese women's newspaper (*Nữ Giới Chung*, 1918; *Phụ Nữ Tân Văn*, 1929), identified by its dish name, newspaper, issue (Số báo) and publication date.

**Action** (Động từ)
The cooking verbs of an Original Recipe: the physical things done to the ingredient (wash, pound, braise…).

**Nguyên văn**
The verbatim original text of an Original Recipe. A Re-cooked Recipe copies it almost word-for-word.

**Poetic Imagery**
A short natural image (e.g. *sương sớm*, *tiếng nước sôi*) chosen at random and used only in how the dish is enjoyed (Cách thưởng thức).

**Re-cooked Recipe**
The output: an Original Recipe re-written with the Emotion as its main ingredient, followed by how to enjoy it and a citation of the Original Recipe.
